# Ironsight Armory

A browser firearms simulator built with Three.js (r128). Everything is procedural: weapon models, textures, soldiers, maps and audio.

- **383 real weapons** from 1888 to today across five eras, including 26 prototypes and experimental designs (G11, TKB-059, Gyrojet, SPIW, OICW…), with real calibres, rates of fire, capacities and muzzle velocities.
- **Psycho Arsenal**: a sixth, fictional era of 21 invented weapons, split into *Feasible* (twin-stack PDW, belt-fed shotgun, integrally suppressed .338, 8-bore breacher, duplex revolver, needle rifle, salvo rifle, smart SMG, gyrojet carbine, FRAG-12 auto-shotgun) and *Overkill* (handheld minigun with spin-up, charge-up coilgun, 7-barrel pepperbox, 20 mm revolver rifle, 30 mm grenade machine gun, twin .50, .50 BMG hand-cannon, seeker-rocket swarm pod, 14.5 mm thermite rifle, 4-bore double rifle). New mechanics: explosive rounds, seeker rounds, rotary spin-up, coil charge, heat-glowing shrouds. Unique attachments, ammo and finishes, two new factions and two new battle maps (Foundry Nine, Blacksite Omega).
- **Soldiers** with lathed anatomical bodies, faces, gloved or bare hands and per-era kit; a phase-based gait with strafing and backpedalling, kneeling, crouch-walking, low-ready, tactical sprint, reload hand paths, hit flinches and five staged death animations that drop the weapon.
- **Armory** with a live 3D showroom and nine attachment slots (optic, muzzle, barrel, underbarrel, lug/side rail, magazine, stock, ammunition, finish), gated by era and weapon, including period items such as the Pedersen Device, Maxim silencer, ZF39, PU and M84 scopes.
- **Firing range** out to 1,000 m with 26 target types: balloons, army pop-ups, shoot/no-shoot turners, a steel spinner, a pendulum swinger, a quadcopter drone, laminated glass, an exploding target, paper, IPSC, steel gongs, poppers, a mover, clay pigeons, plate racks, dueling trees, hostage targets, walking enemies, a barrier penetration test, 14 vehicles with mannequin crews (armour thickness, slope and calibre-based penetration), ballistic gel, a gel torso with skeleton, NIJ armour plates, bottles and era mannequins. Drag, gravity, crosswind and zeroing are simulated; the panel reports time of flight, impact velocity, energy, drop and group size.
- **Kit locker**: 239 items across ten slots (uniform, helmet, face, night vision, headset, armour carrier, plates, gloves, boots, pack) from armies worldwide, WW1 to today, with 19 national presets. Every item is modelled to its real outline: helmet shells with their own cut lines, rails, shrouds, covers and chin straps; SAPI-cut plate bags, cummerbunds, collars and period-correct pouch layouts; NVGs hanging from the shroud; gas masks, headsets, lofted boots and packs with shoulder straps. Protection is computed per zone against real calibres, and the kit's weight slows you down.
- **Test your kit**: "Test it on a mannequin" puts a dummy wearing your exact kit on the range, facing front, rear, side or quarter. Shoot it to see which plates, soft armour, helmets and visors stop which rounds; stopped hits are marked grey and penetrations dark.
- **Lasers and night vision**: PEQ-15, PEQ-2, DBAL and laser grips project a working beam and dot that tighten hip fire. **L** cycles visible, IR, light and combined modes. **J** toggles NVG (monocular, binocular or quad tubes, green or white phosphor); IR lasers are only visible through NVG.
- **Solo missions** (the game's combat mode): clear terrorists from eight structures — a cargo ship with its quay, an oil rig with a second platform, a consulate, the Pier 9 warehouse, a night train, the Hotel Meridian, a hijacked airliner with its hangar and a walled desert compound — each set in a wide outlying area (up to 190 m across) at dawn, day, dusk or night. Up to 27 hostiles in six roles (riflemen, machine gunners, marksmen, breachers, Juggernauts, a cell leader) wear real kit resolved by the same ballistic model as yours. They notice you gradually (distance, stance, movement, darkness, muzzle flash), hear footsteps and gunfire, call out to each other, search, patrol, duck behind cover between bursts, fight worse when wounded and throw grenades when you hide. The dead stay down and drop their weapons: **F** picks one up or swaps it, **K** drops yours. The Armory's *Test in a mission* button deploys straight into an operation with that gun.

Open `index.html` through any static web server (the page loads `js/*.js`). Press **H** in game for controls.
