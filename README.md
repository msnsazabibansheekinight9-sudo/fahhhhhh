# Ironsight Armory

A browser firearms simulator built with Three.js (r128). Everything is procedural: weapon models, textures, soldiers, maps and audio.

- **304 weapons** from 1914 to today across five eras, including 26 prototypes and experimental designs (G11, TKB-059, Gyrojet, SPIW, OICW…), with real calibres, rates of fire, capacities and muzzle velocities.
- **Psycho Arsenal**: a sixth, fictional era of 21 invented weapons, split into *Feasible* (twin-stack PDW, belt-fed shotgun, integrally suppressed .338, 8-bore breacher, duplex revolver, needle rifle, salvo rifle, smart SMG, gyrojet carbine, FRAG-12 auto-shotgun) and *Overkill* (handheld minigun with spin-up, charge-up coilgun, 7-barrel pepperbox, 20 mm revolver rifle, 30 mm grenade machine gun, twin .50, .50 BMG hand-cannon, seeker-rocket swarm pod, 14.5 mm thermite rifle, 4-bore double rifle). New mechanics: explosive rounds, seeker rounds, rotary spin-up, coil charge, heat-glowing shrouds. Unique attachments, ammo and finishes, two new factions and two new battle maps (Foundry Nine, Blacksite Omega).
- **Soldiers** with lathed anatomical bodies, faces, gloved or bare hands and per-era kit; a phase-based gait with strafing and backpedalling, kneeling, crouch-walking, low-ready, tactical sprint, reload hand paths, hit flinches and five staged death animations that drop the weapon.
- **Armory** with a live 3D showroom and nine attachment slots (optic, muzzle, barrel, underbarrel, lug/side rail, magazine, stock, ammunition, finish), gated by era and weapon, including period items such as the Pedersen Device, Maxim silencer, ZF39, PU and M84 scopes.
- **Firing range** out to 1,000 m: paper, IPSC, steel gongs, poppers, a mover, clay pigeons, plate racks, dueling trees, hostage targets, walking enemies, a barrier penetration test, 14 vehicles with mannequin crews (armour thickness, slope and calibre-based penetration), ballistic gel, a gel torso with skeleton, NIJ armour plates, bottles and era mannequins. Drag, gravity, crosswind and zeroing are simulated; the panel reports time of flight, impact velocity, energy, drop and group size.
- **5v5 battles**: two maps per era, four enemy tiers (Recruit → Elite) with different reaction times, aim, armour and tactics; factions carry their own national arsenals.

Open `index.html` through any static web server (the page loads `js/*.js`). Press **H** in game for controls.
