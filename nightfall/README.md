# Nightfall at Ashgrove

An original survival-horror game that runs in the browser (Three.js r128). Everything is generated by code: character models, animations, textures, the manor, the music and every sound effect.

## Story
October 1998. Officer Mara Voss's helicopter goes down near the Velgen Pharmaceuticals estate, and she ends up trapped inside Ashgrove Manor. Her partner Teo Ramirez is missing. Dr. Elias Crane's "Lazarus" agent has taken over the house.

## What's in it
- **Six areas**: the main hall with twin staircases, the dining room, the library, the north corridor, the guard room (a safe room) and the Lazarus laboratory.
- **Enemies**, each with its own procedural rig and AI:
  - Zombies, with eight looks (butler, maid, scientist, guard, police, guest, cook, gardener). They shamble, grab and bite, stagger, get knocked down and get back up. Headshots can pop heads, and leg shots knock them down.
  - Infected hounds that circle, then leap.
  - The **Skinner**, which is blind and hunts by sound. Walk past it; running and gunfire bring it to you.
  - The **Warden**, an unkillable stalker that smashes through a wall, follows you from room to room and opens doors. It can only be stunned, and it won't enter safe rooms.
  - **The Lazarus Form**, the final boss, with two phases, slam and sweep attacks, eyes you can shoot out and an exposed heart.
- **Weapons**: an M19 handgun (crits when your aim is steady), an M37 pump shotgun, a Bulldog .50 magnum and a knife. Ammo is scarce.
- **Classic systems**: typewriter saves, herbs (which can be combined), first aid spray, key items, files to read, a map, an ECG health display, quick turn and cutscenes you can skip.
- **Ending**: a self-destruct escape, followed by a rank screen.

## Controls
WASD to move, Shift to run, mouse to look, right mouse to aim, left mouse to fire (knife when not aiming), R to reload, E to interact, Q to quick turn, 1/2/3 to switch weapons, H to heal, F for the flashlight, Tab for inventory, M for the map, Esc to pause.

## Run it
Serve this folder with any static web server and open `index.html`.
