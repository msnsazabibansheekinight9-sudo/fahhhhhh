# Palmetto Heat

An open-city heist shooter for the browser, built with Three.js (r128). It's inspired by co-op heist shooters and sun-soaked open-world crime games, with original names, maps and designs. Every model, texture, animation and sound is generated in code; there are no asset files.

Open `heist/index.html` from a local server (e.g. `python3 -m http.server`, then visit `/heist/`).

## What's in it

- **Palmetto Bay**: an 8×8-block coastal city with a downtown of towers, an art-deco neon strip, suburbs, docks, a beach, a pier and the ocean. Traffic, pedestrians, palm trees, street lights, neon signs and a 24-minute day/night cycle.
- **Every ordinary building can be entered.** Interiors are built when you walk up: lobbies with reception, columns and mailboxes; floors of apartments (living room, bedroom, kitchenette, wardrobe); office floors (desks, meeting rooms, manager offices); ground-floor shops and stock rooms. A switchback staircase connects up to six floors. Steal cash, jewelry boxes, laptops, TVs, paintings and wall safes. Residents who see you call the police.
- **28 heist contracts across 15 building types**: banks, jewelers, an art gallery and museum, a nightclub, dockside warehouses, an evidence locker, a penthouse and a mansion, a casino, a lab, the Federal Reserve, a mall, a meth cook, an armored truck, a rail yard, an office block and corner stores. Each is a real, walkable building in the city.
- **Custom jobs**: pick any target, the loot, the bag count and extra guards, then plan it like a contract.
- **Stealth**: casing unmasked, masking up (hold X), guard and camera detection, pagers, body bags, shouting civilians down and tying them up, keycards from managers, vault time locks, laser grids, ECMs.
- **Loud**: drills that jam, C4, hacks, police assault waves with control/assault phases, specials (shield, zapper, ninja, medic, marksman, juggernaut), AI crew who revive you, hostage trades and custody.
- **After every job the police chase you.** Shake them, wipe out the pursuit, respray your car or lie low at the safehouse. Get busted and half the take is seized.
- **Notoriety**: your danger level rises with every heist, bringing more patrols, quicker recognition and heavier pursuit.
- **196 weapons** (with akimbo pairs) across rifles, shotguns, LMGs, snipers, SMGs, pistols and specials (grenade launchers, rockets, flamethrowers, miniguns, bows, crossbows, a saw), plus 30 melee weapons and 8 throwables.
- **Working optics**: scopes render a live magnified view through the lens with a mil-dot reticle; a thermal sight shows people white-hot; red dots and holo sights project reticles.
- **Workshop**: 78 mods across 10 slots that change both stats and the model, plus 18 paint finishes. 40 mask shapes × 16 materials × 20 patterns × 20 colour sets.
- **Progression**: 90 skills in 5 trees, 12 perk decks, 7 armours, 8 deployables, levels to 100, infamy ranks, PAYDAY card drops, achievements.
- **The underworld**: a fence, an arms dealer, an informant, a crooked cop, a doctor, a spray shop, a mask tailor and a car dealer. Gangs hold turf. The *Palmetto Bay Herald* comes out every day; some stories are nonsense and some are tips that change pay, guard counts or police patrols.
- Synthwave score that follows the action, three car radio stations and synthesised sound effects.

## Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Move / sprint / jump / crouch | WASD / Shift / Space / C |
| Shoot / aim / reload | LMB / RMB / R |
| Weapons | 1, 2, Q or mouse wheel |
| Mask up | Hold X |
| Interact / shout / enter car | F (tap or hold) |
| Throw bag or throwable | G |
| Melee / deployable / mark | V / E / T |
| Phone / map / newspaper / career | P / M / N / Tab |
| In a car | WASD, Space handbrake, R radio, mouse to shoot your secondary |

Progress is saved in your browser's local storage.
