# Rifle Rivals

A browser recreation of **RIVALS**, the Roblox first-person shooter by Nosniy Games — the
one most people mean when they say "rifle rivals". Built with Three.js (r128) and nothing
else: every weapon, character, map, texture and sound in it is generated by the code in
`js/`, so the whole game is an `index.html` and ten modules.

Open `index.html` and play. No build, no install, no server needed.

```
cd rivals && python3 -m http.server 8000     # or just open index.html
```

## What this is, and what it is not

This is a **recreation built from the public record**, not a copy of the real game's files.
Roblox place files, meshes, textures, audio and the exact server code are not public, so
nothing here is extracted from them and nothing here is claimed to be theirs.

What *is* faithful is everything the community has written down and everything that can be
measured from playing: the weapon roster and its key costs, damage, crit damage, fire rates
and magazine sizes; the mode list and the first-to-five round format; the movement rules
that the game is actually built around; and Roblox's own physics constants, which are used
verbatim. Where the fan wikis disagree on a number — they do on the Assault Rifle, the
Shotgun, the Sniper and the Grenade Launcher — the figure quoted by the most sources is
used and the alternatives are noted in the comment next to that weapon in `js/weapons.js`.

Two things are judgement calls rather than documented facts, and they are marked as such:
health does not regenerate inside a round (it resets between rounds), and in the respawn
modes a kill heals you 25. Both exist because something had to fill the gap, not because a
source says so.

## The game

**Modes** — Team Duel (1v1 to 5v5, elimination rounds, first to five round wins), Ranked
(the same at 1v1/2v2/3v3, with rating on the line), Free For All, Team Deathmatch, Gun Game
(fourteen-rung ladder, a kill promotes you, a melee kill demotes your victim) and
Juggernaut (500 health and an LMG; kill the juggernaut and the title transfers to you).

**Maps** — Arena, Big Arena, Bridge, Crossroads, Big Crossroads, Station, Onyx, Dimension,
Splash and Backrooms. Each is deterministic from its name, so a map always builds the same
way, and each defines its own spawns, kill plane, sky and light rig.

**Weapons** — fifteen, with their key prices:

| Weapon | Class | Keys | Damage | Crit | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fists | melee | free | 20 | 25 | grants a double jump |
| Assault Rifle | primary | free | 13 | 15 | falls to 3 past 200 studs |
| Bow | primary | 15 | 35 | 100 | charge to full, grants a double jump |
| Shotgun | primary | 20 | 8 × 10 | — | 80 inside a doorway |
| Knife | melee | 20 | 40 | 55 | |
| Burst Rifle | primary | 25 | 20 × 3 | 24 | two clean bursts is a kill |
| Revolver | secondary | 25 | 35 | 70 | six shots |
| RPG | primary | 25 | 50 + splash | — | rocket jumps, hurts you |
| SMG | secondary | 35 | 11 | 13 | 820 rpm |
| LMG | primary | 50 | 14 | 17 | 60 rounds, 4s reload |
| Battle Axe | melee | 50 | 50 | 70 | 0.70s swing, lunges |
| Sniper | primary | 75 | 50 | 150 | 1.5s between shots, 5/10 |
| Freeze Ray | secondary | 250 | 8 | 10 | slows what it hits |
| Grenade Launcher | primary | 425 | 40 + splash | — | four bouncing bombs |
| Energy Rifle | primary | 425 | 20 | 26 | beams ricochet three times |

**Keys** are earned the way the real game pays: 2 a kill, 1 more for a crit, up to 4 more
on a streak, 5 for a round win and 35 for a match win. They buy weapons, charms and skins.
Rating moves on ranked results and runs Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond →
Master → Rival. All of it is saved in `localStorage`.

## Movement

RIVALS is a movement shooter, so this is the part that had to be right:

- **Slide** — crouch while moving for a boost that decays back to walking speed.
- **Slide jump** — jump out of the slide and the boosted speed goes with you. This is the
  skill the whole game is built on.
- **Slide cancel** — jump inside the first 0.18s of a slide and you keep *all* the
  momentum, which is the forward hop good players chain across a map.
- **Double jump** — granted by the Fists and the Bow, and by nothing else.
- **Triple jump** — swap between Fists and Bow in mid-air and the air jump comes back, once
  per airtime.
- **Rocket jump** — fire an RPG or a grenade at your feet; the blast launches you and the
  self-damage is real.
- **Air strafe** — turn into a strafe key in the air to gain speed, so a chain holds.

Gravity is 196.2 studs/s², a jump leaves the ground at 50 studs/s and a character is a 5.25
stud R6 body with its eyes at 4.6 — Roblox's own numbers, used so the feel transfers.

## Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Move | `W` `A` `S` `D` |
| Jump / double jump | `Space` |
| Slide | `Shift` or `C` |
| Fire / scope | `LMB` / `RMB` |
| Reload | `R` |
| Weapons | `1` `2` `3`, `Q` for last, wheel to cycle |
| Scoreboard | `Tab` |
| Camera | `V` |
| Menu | `Esc` |

## How it is put together

| file | what it owns |
|---|---|
| `js/core.js` | renderer, input, synth audio, procedural textures, particles, decals, tracers, the swept-AABB collision world |
| `js/weapons.js` | the weapon table, ballistics, hitboxes, projectiles, explosions |
| `js/avatar.js` | R6 characters, faces, charms, the fall-apart death, and all fifteen weapon models |
| `js/vmodels.js` | first-person rigs: the same models with hands on them, plus sway, recoil, reload and scope |
| `js/maps.js` | the map pool and the loader |
| `js/movement.js` | the character controller and the local player |
| `js/bots.js` | opponents: target choice, aim with human error, cover, movement tech |
| `js/modes.js` | match flow, scoring, keys, ranks, the save file |
| `js/ui.js` | HUD, kill feed, scoreboard, lobby, shop, loadout, settings |
| `js/game.js` | boot and the frame loop |

Weapons are modelled once, in weapon space, and used for both the viewmodel and the model
in everyone else's hands, so first and third person can never disagree about what someone
is holding.

`artifact.html` is the same page with the page skeleton stripped, for publishing as a
hosted artifact. Regenerate it with `node make-artifact.js` after editing `index.html`.

## Checks

```
node check.js            # full run: parses every module, boots the page, plays every mode
node check.js --syntax   # parse only
```

57 assertions. They check the weapon table against the documented figures, build all ten
maps and confirm every spawn has ground under it, measure walk speed, slide speed, slide
jump carry and jump height against the Roblox constants, fire real shots at a real target
to confirm 13 to the body and 150 on a sniper crit, verify walls stop bullets and energy
beams do not, run fifteen seconds of bots through every mode, play a duel to a 5-win
finish, and exercise the shop, the save file and every menu tab.

The page loads Three.js from cdnjs. If the machine running the checks has no outbound
network, point `THREE_LOCAL` at a local `three.min.js` (r128) and the check serves it
instead:

```
THREE_LOCAL=/path/to/three.min.js node check.js
```
